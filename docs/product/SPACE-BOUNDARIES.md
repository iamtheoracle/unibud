# UNIBUD Space Boundaries

## Shared platform, separate experiences

The ecosystem may share identity, navigation, search, messaging, notifications, media infrastructure, trust and safety, and authorised AI assistance. Each space must retain its own purpose, interface conventions, data ownership, access rules, and lifecycle.

## Quad versus Connect

Quad is a distinct discovery space and must not be merged into Connect or treated as a renamed Connect surface. Preserve its separate purpose, navigation, feature set, interaction model, permissions, privacy settings, safety boundaries, and lifecycle. Its exact founder-approved feature contract is not present in the currently inspected canonical repository documents; recover that record before implementing Quad-specific behaviour. Do not invent a substitute concept or infer its functionality from Connect.


Bud owns academic learning and student support. Board is a campus workspace inside Bud, not a news feed. It maps real school locations and gives access to school-specific resources and services. Academic lectures, seminars, podcasts, and educational recordings belong in Bud's academic media/library context.

Square is general entertainment and creator media. Academic media must not be mixed into Square merely because both contain video or audio.

## Connect versus Wayward

Connect helps people discover people, communities, institutions, creators, opportunities, and services. Wayward helps people discover, create, organise, and participate in experiences. An activity may be discoverable through Connect while its planning, participants, schedule, and operations remain in Wayward.

## Riff and Lync

Riff supports lightweight open conversation. Lync supports purposeful engagement and relationship continuity. Neither should become a duplicate of the main Connect experience.

## Concierge and The Quarter

Concierge is the conversational service interface and workflow coordinator. The Quarter is the marketplace domain that owns product/service listings, seller terms, orders, fulfilment, refunds, and disputes. Concierge may initiate a Quarter workflow only through authenticated, authorised APIs and explicit confirmation.

## Operations

UNIBUD Operations is internal-only. Consumer participation does not grant access to staff tools, dispatch, incident records, fleet data, or internal administration.
