# UNIBUD — Master Product Vision

## Purpose

UNIBUD brings together useful digital spaces and real-world services without forcing them into one generic social feed. People should be able to find the right place, understand what it is for, and move between connected experiences without losing context or control.

## Product principles

- **One ecosystem, distinct spaces:** each space owns its purpose, interaction patterns, data rules, and safety model.
- **Simple on the surface, capable underneath:** avoid crowded navigation and unnecessary dashboards; complexity belongs in dependable systems.
- **Connection without coercion:** discovery and participation must not require forced friendship, matching, or public exposure.
- **Real-world accountability:** physical services, events, transport, payments, and fulfilment need accountable people, records, and operational controls.
- **User agency:** visibility, consent, communication, memory, and consequential actions remain under clear user control.
- **Evidence over claims:** planned, implemented, tested, and verified are different states.

## Cross-space capabilities

Identity, profiles, permissions, messaging, notifications, search, media, payments, bookings, trust and safety, and AI assistance may be shared as platform capabilities. Sharing infrastructure must not collapse domain boundaries or automatically grant access across spaces.

## Profile families

- Personal profiles represent individuals.
- Creator profiles represent publishing identities and creator work.
- Page/community profiles represent institutions, organisations, brands, and communities.

One account may own or manage multiple profile types. Management permissions are scoped to the relevant profile and never imply access to the owner's personal account.

## Release philosophy

The complete ecosystem remains the target. Delivery is staged by dependency and risk, not by deleting product scope. Regulated financial features, adult experiences, professional support, and physical operations must not be represented as production-ready before their legal, safety, provider, and operational requirements are satisfied.
