# UNIBUD Operations — Requirements

UNIBUD Operations coordinates services and experiences that require people, locations, vehicles, suppliers, or physical fulfilment. It is an internal workforce system, not a consumer-facing social space.

## Core records

- Workforce identity, role, status, region, permissions, availability, training, and assignment history.
- Experience and service operating plans, locations, schedules, capacity, suppliers, and safety requirements.
- Vehicle ownership/control, registration, capacity, assigned driver, availability, maintenance, insurance, permits, and roadworthiness.
- Assignments, dispatch approvals, check-in/out, fulfilment evidence, incidents, expenses, and reconciliation.

## Operational lifecycle

Request → eligibility and risk review → operating plan → people/resources → approval → booking and confirmation → participant communication → delivery → incident handling if needed → financial reconciliation → closure.

## Authority

AI may draft plans, surface conflicts, and recommend resources. Authorised humans approve dispatch, spend, safety exceptions, refunds, and other consequential operational decisions.

## Safety and accountability

No dispatch if required driver or vehicle documentation is missing or expired. Field representatives must have clear lawful roles, accountable supervision, and safety procedures. No covert surveillance, impersonation, or unaccountable enforcement.

## Evidence

Track assignments, approvals, provider confirmations, operational events, incidents, and closure. Never mark a physical service completed solely because an AI agent generated a completion message.
