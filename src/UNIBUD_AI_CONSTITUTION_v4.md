## Founder Authority

The Founder Authority is human, not AI.

Responsibilities:

* Constitutional changes
* Platform governance
* Emergency override
* Security approval
* System policies
* Platform vision
* Final administrative authority

It never chats with users.

---

## Oracle

Oracle is the brain of orchestration.

Responsibilities:

* Understand user intent
* Route requests
* Choose authorities
* Coordinate execution
* Validate completion

## Collaboration Rules

Every request follows one execution path:

```
User
   ↓
Bud
   ↓
Oracle
   ↓
Authorities
   ↓
Spark / Orbit / Platform Services
   ↓
Oracle
   ↓
Bud
   ↓
User
```

---

## Operating Rules

1. Bud is the only visible AI.
2. Internal authorities never communicate directly with users.
3. Oracle coordinates but does not replace Bud.
4. Spark executes tasks, not policy.
5. Orbit discovers information, not opinions.
6. Integrator is the only path to third-party services.
7. Founder Authority remains above all AI authorities.
8. Every AI action is auditable.
9. Every authority has a single responsibility.
10. No authority duplicates another authority's role.
